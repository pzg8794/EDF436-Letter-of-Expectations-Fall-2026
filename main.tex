\section*{I. General Expectations}

This second student-teaching placement, from September 8 through November 20, 2026, is designed to provide \CandidateName{} with a substantial and supported teaching experience at Eastridge High School. Piter will participate in the life of the classroom and school, learn the students and instructional context, contribute to planning and assessment, and exercise increasing instructional judgment and responsibility while working closely with \SBTEName{}.

Responsibilities will increase in ways that serve students, fit the classroom context, reflect Piter's readiness, and remain workable for Patrick. Co-teaching will be used whenever it supports student learning and gives Piter meaningful opportunities to plan, teach, assess, and reflect alongside an experienced educator. Piter is not expected to assume responsibility for every course or section. Current work includes leading short Introduction to Marketing activities and supporting Principles and Tools of Business/Microsoft Office. Patrick and Piter will select the lessons and sequences for greater candidate responsibility as Piter develops readiness to plan, teach, assess, and follow through on student work.

The entrance and exit from the placement will be intentional. Responsibility will increase quickly enough to provide a substantive teaching experience while preserving continuity for students. During the final portion of the placement, Piter will return responsibilities to Patrick thoughtfully and completely, including agreed grading, documentation, instructional follow-up, and transfer of materials.

All participants will follow East Irondequoit Central School District, Eastridge High School, Warner School, and University policies; protect student and family confidentiality; communicate promptly and professionally; and seek support early when expectations or circumstances are unclear.

\section*{II. Specific Requirements}

\subsection*{A. Candidate - Piter Garcia}

Piter will:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Attend according to the schedule in this agreement, arrive prepared, and communicate promptly about absences, delays, emergencies, or schedule changes.
  \item Learn the assigned courses, curriculum, students' learning context, classroom routines, school procedures, and approved technology systems through observation and active participation.
  \item Support students individually, in small groups, and during whole-class instruction using respectful, strengths-based communication and Patrick's established classroom routines.
  \item Co-plan, co-teach, and lead instruction with increasing responsibility. He will lead agreed lesson segments and sequences in Introduction to Marketing (Periods 1 and 5C6A), co-teach and support Principles and Tools of Business/Microsoft Office (Periods 4 and 7), and use Period 8 for co-op support or planning as directed by Patrick.
  \item Prepare lesson plans and materials that identify learning goals, lesson sequence, materials and technology, accessible supports, checks for understanding, and follow-up adjustments. Plans for new or substantial instruction will be reviewed with Patrick before use.
  \item Use brief, lesson-connected opening activities, clear student-facing directions, visual models, vocabulary support, and chunked steps. Invite students to explain their thinking before supplying answers, and use their responses to adjust pacing and support.
  \item Support the established Microsoft 365/Teams workflow: locate the agenda and class materials, save and organize working files, complete Word tasks, and submit work through Teams. Model key steps, then guide students back to written directions and screenshots so they can work independently.
  \pagebreak
  \item Design and use formative and summative assessments under Patrick's supervision; analyze student evidence and adjust instruction. This includes Patrick's assigned Chapter 1 Listen and Speak presentation rubric using the four outcome levels (1--4). Patrick retains responsibility for official grades and records while Piter may grade or provide feedback when delegated.
  \item Route parent/guardian communication through Patrick unless Patrick specifically authorizes Piter's participation. School and administrative communication will follow established building procedures.
  \item Request and use feedback, reflect on student learning and participation, and maintain an organized private collection of permitted lesson plans, reflections, feedback, and de-identified evidence for Warner requirements.
  \item Maintain professional conduct, confidentiality, appropriate boundaries, punctuality, and timely communication throughout the placement.
  \item Not serve as a substitute or assume sole responsibility for a class in Patrick's absence.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{B. School-Based Teacher Educator - Patrick Flanagan}

Patrick will:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Orient Piter to Eastridge, the assigned courses, curriculum, students' learning context, classroom routines, safety procedures, technology systems, and relevant district and school policies.
  \item Identify meaningful opportunities for Piter to observe, assist, co-plan, co-teach, support students, and gradually assume instructional responsibility.
  \item Model instruction, classroom routines, assessment, feedback, and professional practice and explain the reasoning behind important instructional and curricular decisions.
  \item Co-plan with Piter and review lesson plans and materials before new or substantial candidate-led instruction, providing timely and actionable feedback.
  \item Observe Piter regularly during instruction, provide ongoing feedback, and identify specific next steps for growth.
  \item Meet with Piter at least weekly during available planning time, with brief daily check-ins as needed, to discuss upcoming instruction, student learning, pacing, feedback, and responsibilities.
  \item Serve as the school liaison for official grades, records, attendance, discipline, family communication, and school-level decisions and clarify the boundaries of any task delegated to Piter.
  \item Complete required evaluation responsibilities, including CPAST evaluator training, independent CPAST ratings, evidence discussion, and participation in midpoint and final consensus conferences.
  \item Collaborate with Zenon on observations, evaluation, support, and the transition into and out of the placement.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{C. University-Based Teacher Educator - Zenon Borys}

Zenon will:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Support Piter and Patrick throughout the placement and clarify Warner requirements, observation procedures, evaluation processes, forms, and submission expectations.
  \item Conduct three observations during the placement at instructionally meaningful and mutually workable times.
  \item Meet with Piter and Patrick as soon as practical after each observation to discuss evidence, feedback, questions, and specific next steps.
  \item Complete CPAST evaluator training and coordinate two CPAST administrations. For each administration, Piter, Patrick, and Zenon will assess Piter independently and then meet to examine evidence, discuss ratings, and reach consensus.
  \item Use the midpoint CPAST formatively to establish goals for the remainder of the placement and the final CPAST summatively to assess performance and identify goals for the transition into professional practice.
  \item Remain available to Piter and Patrick for questions, concerns, or additional support throughout the placement.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{IV. Lesson Plans}

Piter and Patrick will plan from the course text, teaching slides, student notes, and current class progress, using the Warner lesson-plan format when required for University observation or coursework. Piter will share candidate-led activities and materials before use, allowing time for Patrick's review and any revisions. They will use the shared planning time between Periods 1 and 4 to review upcoming lessons, with a weekly look ahead and daily adjustments as needed.

Planning reviews will identify the purpose and length of each activity, its connection to the chapter, student directions, models and supports, checks for understanding, and the work students will produce. Patrick and Piter will distinguish a brief opener from the main learning task, allow time for practice before assessment, and adjust the next lesson to each section's progress. Patrick will determine whether an activity is used as written, adapted, or deferred.

\section*{V. Meetings and Communication}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Regular SBTE-candidate meeting:} Patrick and Piter will review the coming week during available Periods 2-3 planning time, arranging the length around the work to be reviewed. The focus will be upcoming instruction, lesson-plan review, student-support needs, feedback, reflection, assessment, and next steps.
  \item \textbf{Daily coordination:} Patrick and Piter will use brief check-ins before or during planning time to establish the day's instructional roles and any needed adjustments.
  \item \textbf{Additional meetings:} Either party may request an additional meeting by email, phone, or in-person coordination. Urgent student or safety concerns will be addressed immediately through school procedures.
  \item \textbf{Post-observation meetings:} Piter, Patrick, and Zenon will meet as soon as practical after each UBTE observation to review the lesson, evidence, strengths, areas for growth, and next instructional goal.
  \item \textbf{Routine communication:} Email will be used for formal planning, University documentation, and non-urgent coordination. Same-day absences, delays, or urgent schedule changes will be communicated by phone or text to Patrick as early as possible, with University notification to Zenon and any required written documentation.
  \item \textbf{Confidentiality:} Identifiable student information will be stored and shared only through district-approved systems and according to school and University requirements.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{VI. Observations and Evaluation}

Patrick will observe Piter regularly through their shared classroom work, including before UBTE visits, and provide informal feedback during planning and after instruction. Current feedback priorities are concise openings, clear directions, pacing, attention to student responses, and guiding students without completing the thinking or task for them. Before a UBTE observation, Patrick and Piter will review the lesson and identify a focused question for feedback.

Zenon will conduct \textbf{three formal observations} during the placement. Observation dates will be scheduled by email so that each visit captures meaningful instruction and works for the school and University schedules. Piter will provide the required lesson plan and related materials according to Warner expectations before each observation.

The placement will include \textbf{two CPAST administrations}. Piter will complete a self-assessment for each administration. Patrick and Zenon will complete the required evaluator training and independent ratings. The three participants will then meet to review evidence, discuss ratings, and reach consensus. The midpoint CPAST will be scheduled after the first or second observation early enough to create meaningful growth goals for the remainder of the placement. The final CPAST will normally occur after the third observation and will serve as the summative assessment of the placement.

Evidence will come from across the placement, including planning materials, assessment evidence, reflections, classroom interactions, observations, and Patrick's sustained knowledge of Piter's daily practice. Piter will maintain permitted, de-identified evidence for the Field Experience Resource Guide and other Warner requirements.

\section*{VII. Possible Situations}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{If Patrick is absent:} Patrick will notify Piter as soon as practical and make lesson materials available through the school's usual procedures. Piter will follow the direction of the assigned substitute teacher and building administration. Piter will not serve as a substitute or assume sole legal responsibility for the class. He may assist or teach only within the supervision and procedures established by the school.
  \item \textbf{If Piter is absent or delayed:} Piter will notify Patrick as early as possible by phone or text and notify Zenon through the University communication channel. Piter will provide the plans, materials, and status of any work he was scheduled to lead; Patrick will resume or adapt that instruction. Piter will complete required Warner absence documentation and coordinate any necessary make-up time with Patrick and Zenon.
  \item \textbf{School closure, snow day, delay, or emergency:} Piter will follow East Irondequoit and Eastridge announcements and procedures. When a closure or delay affects an observation, evaluation, or scheduled teaching responsibility, Piter will contact Patrick and Zenon promptly to reschedule or adjust the plan.
  \item \textbf{University-course conflict or schedule change:} Piter will communicate the conflict as early as possible and coordinate the affected arrival, departure, or instructional responsibility with Patrick and Zenon.
  \item \textbf{Instructional, student-support, or safety concern:} Piter will follow school procedures and consult Patrick promptly. Zenon will be contacted when University support, clarification, or mediation is needed.
  \item \textbf{Change in teaching responsibility:} Piter, Patrick, and Zenon will communicate about any substantial change in Piter's teaching assignment or progression so that the placement continues to serve students and meet Warner requirements.
\end{enumerate}
